About

Dr. Liang’s career has moved across medicine, biochemistry, medical microbiology, viral informatics and computational biology. His current research uses omics, bioinformatics and large-scale data approaches to investigate viruses, host susceptibility, viral evolution and strategies for treating infectious diseases.

A career built by connecting disciplines.
Medical training was not discarded when the work moved into research, and biological science was not replaced when computation became more important. Each stage added another way of understanding disease.

Family-provided background: Before relocating to Canada, Dr. Liang practiced as a neurologist in Jilin, China. His public University of Manitoba profile verifies his MD in Internal Medicine from Jilin University; the specific neurology role is included here as family-provided biographical context.

Research Focus

Viral genomics & bioinformatics

Applying sequencing, computational analysis and large biological datasets to characterize viral diversity, evolution and pathogenesis.

HIV drug resistance & surveillance

Early studies helped establish a foundation for next-generation-sequencing-based HIV drug-resistance testing in Canada.

Host–virus interactions

Investigating human genetic determinants and biological responses associated with susceptibility or resistance to infectious disease.

Vaccines & therapeutics

Research includes preclinical HIV/SIV vaccine strategies and computational approaches to infectious-disease treatment.
